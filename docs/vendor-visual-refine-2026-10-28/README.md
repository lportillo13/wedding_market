# Vendor cards, image-led hero and section navigation

The owner identified card text touching borders and an overly boxed card finish, then requested a photo-led profile hero with subtle boxed information, clearer section hover/active feedback and a filter that does not look clipped.

- The owner requested visual alternatives for directory cards before coding, then clarified the final choice by attaching **B, image-overlay cards**, superseding the earlier A selection. Final cards use immersive photos (3:5 on desktop, square on phones) with Lucia categories, light GT Super names, compact city/rating metadata and a profile arrow over a bottom gradient. Long summaries and white card bodies are removed; full descriptions remain on profiles. Actual vendor logos remain, and eligible real listings have an accessible favorite-heart control over the photo. Example listings retain their disabled saving/inquiry behavior. Final reference: [approved directory cards B](selected-cards-b.png).
- Listing cities are read using the existing anonymous public client, selecting only vendor ID, city and region for IDs on the current bounded page. This stays inside the small 30-second public-only directory cache, cleared after location saves; no street/contact fields or new schema are introduced. Card links do not prefetch profile pages while visitors scroll.
- The profile hero uses one full-width cover photo or video. After reviewing the first ivory-panel version, the owner requested removal of that box, then asked for image concepts before further coding. Three desktop/phone concepts were supplied: A cinematic caption, B information strip, C centered editorial. The owner selected **C**. The final cover centers the light vendor name, compact location/rating and actions over a soft full-width gradient, with a filled primary action and quiet underlined secondary actions. Phone actions stack as in the chosen concept. Summary, experience/language/event details and breadcrumbs sit in About; other photos remain in the gallery. Existing quote/save/share restrictions remain. Future design changes should be previewed visually and selected before implementation.
- Section tabs have a warm hover/active background and animated underline, respecting reduced motion. The active tab stays visible by scrolling only its horizontal navigation container.
- The search/filter spans the content width with inset controls, a separate padded button and visible overflow for focus/native controls. Fields use shrinkable grid tracks and stack on phones.
- The owner also requested new typography on About. Its headings, story lead, quotes, mission, journal and final invitation use GT Super Light; body copy uses regular Acumin and the opening accent uses Lucia. Warm shared colors and consistent button/photo corners replace the remaining heavier page-specific typography. Content and section animations are retained.
- At the owner's request, the large explanatory sample notice is removed from profile and directory pages so the actual design can be reviewed without an extra banner. Small directory sample labels, opt-in URLs, noindex and disabled inquiry submission remain.

Three quote-sidebar alternatives were supplied before coding; the owner selected **A, slim floating bar**. The permanent right column and duplicate phone bar are replaced with one small dock on desktop and phone. It appears after the hero, hides when the contact section is reached, preserves guest sign-in/price restrictions and remains absent for vendor viewers. The main content uses the full grid width while About prose keeps a comfortable reading measure. Reference: [approved floating dock](selected-quote-a.png).

Scroll targets, video admission and dock visibility use the combined global-header/profile-menu height. A hero hidden behind the sticky menus therefore stops its media and reveals the quote dock, including immediately after choosing About.

- Vendors choose Photo or Video in Profile → Media. Video can be selected from their existing uploaded profile clips or supplied as a public YouTube, Vimeo or direct HTTPS MP4/WebM URL. Library choices must belong to that vendor's current video assets. Existing upload processing, 50 MB admission limit and three-video quota are unchanged.
- Selection is stored in the existing `extra_info.hero_presentation`; `hero_image` remains the photo/poster so installed native clients continue displaying an image. Deleted/missing video selections fall back to the photo. No schema migration is needed.
- All cover video sources autoplay silently and loop, including YouTube/Vimeo, as explicitly requested by the owner. A pause/play control remains. Reduced motion disables automatic playback; media pauses/unmounts offscreen or in background tabs. External players are admitted only when the cover is visible in the foreground, use privacy-enhanced embeds and have an external source link. Provider embedding restrictions still apply. Supported parameters follow [YouTube's documentation](https://developers.google.com/youtube/player_parameters) and [Vimeo's player documentation](https://developer.vimeo.com/player/sdk/embed).
- `/vendors/example-ritmo-atardecer` demonstrates a YouTube cover using the same wedding film already embedded on the homepage; other examples demonstrate a single photo. The abstract legacy `wedding-demo.mp4` is not used in the final sample. Gallery photos remain available below the hero.
- Held native source accepts the optional video field and adds a Watch cover video action that opens the source in the device player/browser. It retains the photo poster; inline native playback is not added in this pass.
