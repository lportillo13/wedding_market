# FoundersCard visual direction — October 27, 2026

The owner identified https://founderscard.com/ as the desired feeling for Wedding Market. The reference was reviewed in the browser, including its hero and membership panels.

## Direction to carry into Wedding Market

- Large, light editorial serif headings with selective italic lines.
- Immersive photography/video, restrained overlays and clear text contrast.
- Generous space, fewer competing elements and one clear action within each section.
- Ivory and warm neutral surfaces balanced with deep charcoal/brown sections.
- Thin dividers, quiet controls and subtle corner radii rather than decorative card stacks.
- A recognizable wordmark without a location or translated tagline. The owner rejected the Lucia script logo; use light GT Super uppercase lettering for the web wordmark and reserve Lucia for small content accents.
- Preserve the approved navigation structure, readable form labels and existing EN/ES behavior. Account workspaces should remain practical and compact.

The reference's inspected hero heading uses Feature Display and its navigation uses Neue Montreal. These are observations about the reference, not font files obtained. The owner subsequently supplied GT Super, Lucia BT and Acumin Pro files and selected that combination for a sitewide evaluation, with light weights. This supersedes the earlier Ms Claudy / Adobe Garamond direction. No font purchase was made.

The implementation places the existing navigation over the homepage video, adds a controllable video/poster state, spacious discovery/category/magazine sections, staggered entrance motion, keyboard-operated discovery tabs and reduced-motion support. Interior pages share the same typography and warm palette. Website validation and deployment are recorded separately in `../founders-refresh-2026-10-27/README.md`. Native release remains held.
