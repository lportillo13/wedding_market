Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: first-round brand identity comparison board for The Wedding Market, a bilingual wedding vendor marketplace for weddings in Costa Rica. This is a draft for review, not a final approved identity.
Create one exceptionally polished wide landscape graphic design board with three distinct logo proposals arranged in three equal columns on a perfectly flat warm ivory background. Editorial wedding stationery sensibility, quiet luxury, credible marketplace brand. Plenty of breathing room, precise alignment and clean edges. Colors are warm ivory #FBF7F0, champagne #C9AC79, deep cocoa #4B352C, and taupe #8E7766.
Column A labeled "01 / MONOGRAM": a carefully designed original W M serif monogram, balanced and legible, with a restrained interlocking gesture suggesting partnership. No tiny flourishes, no enclosing ornamental crest. Below it the exact brand wordmark "THE WEDDING MARKET" in beautiful editorial serif lettering.
Column B labeled "02 / BOTANICAL": an original simple two-leaf and wedding-arch inspired symbol, abstract and elegant, subtly suggesting Costa Rica without palm-tree tourism cliches. A sophisticated robust silhouette. Below it the same exact brand wordmark.
Column C labeled "03 / WORDMARK": a distinctive high-quality custom serif brand wordmark "THE WEDDING MARKET" with one restrained typographic relationship that suggests connection. Underneath show a compact W-based companion symbol derived from its lettering for tiny contexts.
Each column has a clear large primary logo at the top, a row showing its coherent matching app icon below (one square with ivory background and cocoa symbol and one square with cocoa background and ivory symbol, rounded corners only to simulate the OS), and a small robust favicon example on the last row. Icons contain only their respective symbol, never the full long name. All elements within a column must be the same identity. Show honest small-size recognizability.
Header at upper left exact text "The Wedding Market" and small subheading "IDENTITY EXPLORATIONS". Footer exact text "FIRST CONCEPTS · OCTOBER 2026".
Use flat vector-like shapes, legible text, polished typography. No photographs, no mockup objects, no gradients, no metallic textures, no shadows, no wedding rings, no clipart, no green or teal, no extra slogans, no existing brand logos. Ensure all three solutions remain simple enough to reproduce as a one-color favicon. The visual output should feel like a professional identity designer's considered first presentation, not a template collage.
